## A content-first portfolio project

TheIslah Blog is a practical example of turning a WordPress-style content site into a fast, modern Astro experience. The goal was to preserve content quality while improving performance, maintainability, and the overall reading experience.

The project reflects the kind of work I enjoy most: thoughtful content structure, modern static hosting, and a clear path from migration to launch.

## What mattered most

- Strong content presentation with a calm, readable layout
- Better performance and SEO for a publication-oriented site
- A migration path that keeps the content manageable over time
- A modern deployment setup that is simple to maintain

## Why it fits my profile

This project connects well with my interests in backend reliability, content systems, and practical engineering choices. It also reflects my growing focus on building useful sites with clean architecture and a strong user experience.
